CLINICAL GUIDELINE: MALARIA TREATMENT IN NIGERIA
Version: 2026.1
Publisher: Ministry of Health Nigeria
License: Open Public Access

1. OVERVIEW
Malaria remains a major public health concern in Nigeria. This guideline details the recommended treatment regimens for uncomplicated and severe malaria.

2. UNCOMPLICATED MALARIA
Uncomplicated malaria is defined by the presence of malaria symptoms (fever, chills, headache) without signs of organ dysfunction.
The recommended first-line treatment is Artemether-Lumefantrine (ACT).
- Dosage for adults: Artemether 80mg / Lumefantrine 480mg twice daily for 3 days.
- ACT should be taken with fatty food to enhance absorption.

3. CONTRAINDICATIONS AND WARNINGS
- Artemether-Lumefantrine is contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to artemether or lumefantrine.
- Avoid use in patients with congenital long QT syndrome or severe renal/hepatic impairment.
- Side effects: Headache, dizziness, abdominal pain, and muscle pain.
